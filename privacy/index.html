Privacy Policy

Superimmersive AR (iOS app and App Clip) · Last updated 29 October 2026

Superimmersive AR is made by SUPERIMMERSIVE ART (PTY) LTD. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app and its App Clip do not ask you to sign in, do not collect personal information, and contain no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Network use

The app downloads 3D models from superimmersive.io so you can view them. These are ordinary web requests for model files; they do not include any personal information, and we do not use them to identify you. Like any website, our host may keep standard short-term server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.

Camera

The camera is used only to place 3D models in your room with augmented reality. Camera images are processed on your device and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
