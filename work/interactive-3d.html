Case · Browser 3D

A model you can turn

A jet engine in the browser, with animation and iPhone Quick Look. A house walkthrough sits beside it as a separate product demo.

Open the 3D viewer Homes recordings
  1. Problem

    A still does not let someone turn an object. A property conversation often starts before CAD exists, and a site team may want a 3D view of a real part without a separate manual.

  2. Build

    The interactive web page runs a realtime jet engine: orbit, two animations, and a USDZ for Quick Look on iPhone. Super Immersive Homes turns a brief or rough plan into an editable layout and a 3D walkthrough — a working core, pre-revenue, and not council-ready drawings. Scanning and 3D Gaussian splatting are the capture side. Splatting is pipeline-ready and needs a 360 drone.

  3. Result

    Open the jet viewer. Watch the two Homes recordings. A facilities-style idea — a code on equipment that opens a 3D view — is a prototype the studio can build. It is not a deployed helpdesk, and no municipality is named as a client. The longer capability note is digital twin and QR-linked 3D.
