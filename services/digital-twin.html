Open the 3D viewer

This is a capability page. Superimmersive Art can prototype a view of a real object or place, then a simple way to open it from a phone. It is not a deployed facilities system, not an integration with an asset register, and not a statement that any municipality is a client.

What you can open or read now:

  • Browser 3D viewer — orbit a jet engine, play an animation, or open Quick Look on iPhone.
  • 3D scanning of objects and environments, including twins of products for the web and AR.
  • 3D Gaussian splatting for navigable spaces. The pipeline is ready. A capture needs a 360 drone.
  • WebAR markers and QR-style entry from a printed card.
  • Super Immersive Homes recordings. That product is a design conversation before CAD. It is pre-revenue and it is not a facilities platform.

The longer note, including what a facilities-style proof of concept would and would not be, is site capture and QR-linked 3D. The case note is interactive 3D.
