3D, technical art and interactive web

Assets that run in a headset or a browser, and the pages that present them.

Superimmersive Art produces realtime 3D and the web applications that show it. Technical art for immersive pipelines is the studio’s current paid work. The same stack covers product viewers, configurators, and WebAR.

Detail on the asset side is on technical art and 3D content production. Detail on the browser side is on interactive web development.

What you can commission

  • Realtime assets for VR and training: models, materials, animation, and look-dev that hold up in a headset.
  • Lighting, cleanup, and branding on a scene that is already in progress.
  • Modelling and short visualisation when the brief is a still or a flythrough rather than an app.
  • Interactive web: a product viewer, a configurator, or a WebAR layer. The interactive web page includes a live jet-engine model with orbit controls and Quick Look on iPhone.

When the job starts from a real object or place

  • 3D scanning of objects and environments, cleaned for realtime, print, or XR.
  • 3D Gaussian splatting for photoreal, navigable spaces. The pipeline is ready. A capture needs a 360 drone. Output can also feed Super Immersive Homes.
  • Site capture and QR-linked 3D for a proof of concept that opens a view from a phone. That page does not claim a facilities contract.
