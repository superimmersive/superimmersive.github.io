Service · Industry and education

Immersive and VR training for industry and education

Simulations for procedures that are costly or risky to rehearse on live equipment.

Superimmersive Art builds training simulations for colleges, safety programmes, and in-house technical teams. A project can ship as a headset module, a browser exercise, or a licensed tool the institution keeps.

The point is practical familiarity with equipment, processes, and environments before someone walks into the real facility. That line is also the company’s stated mission for South African education. The same build approach applies to corporate training.

What a commission includes

  • A procedure broken into steps a learner can repeat.
  • The 3D environment and the interaction, built for realtime rather than as a film.
  • A Quest build, a browser build, or both, depending on the classroom.
  • A path to license a finished module to a similar institution. That is the product plan. It is not a catalogue you can buy off the shelf today.

Headset delivery is also described under XR, AR and WebAR app development.

Work you can look at

  • Craniotomy training demo on the homepage, with a Quest APK and a web build.
  • Simulation-based training tools, the product page for licensing and the studio delivery record.
  • VR driving-school simulator. Documentation is complete and the build is in prototype, framed around South African driving standards and the K53 test. It is not a finished school product yet.

Where the track record comes from

The founder has 11+ years in immersive and 3D work. During studio engagements, simulations were delivered for subjects such as fire safety, mining operations, railway safety, diagnostic machines, and vehicle tasks. The organisations are named on the simulation tools page and on the homepage.

That work was delivered across studio and contract roles. It is not a claim that each organisation is a direct client of Superimmersive Art (Pty) Ltd.
