XR, AR and WebAR app development

Headset applications, WebXR, and browser AR from a printed page or a product.

Superimmersive Art designs and builds extended-reality software from George, Western Cape. A commission can be a Meta Quest application, a WebXR experience in the browser, or WebAR that starts from a printed page, a package, or a product photo.

The founder does the technical art and the development. Export technical-art contracts are the studio’s current revenue. Immersive product work is built in the same shop.

What you can commission

  • Quest and OpenXR applications for training, products, and brand experiences.
  • WebXR prototypes that run on a phone or headset without a store listing.
  • WebAR from image markers: books, menus, packaging, cards, retail displays, and vehicles.
  • Interaction design and realtime 3D that can sit next to an existing Unity or web pipeline.

The shorter note on headset and WebXR work is XR app development. Published marker demos are on the AR books and WebAR page.
