Superimmersive is a South African IT/4IR startup specialising in immersive technology, 3D development and interactive training experiences.

The company is Black-owned and youth-owned, with the founder born in 1992.

The founder and director has 11+ years of professional industry experience across technical art, 3D development, software and immersive technology, with experience working with major organisations and international clients.

Black-owned and youth-owned 11+ years professional industry experience George, Western Cape

Direction

Our Mission

Superimmersive's long-term mission is to become an industry leader in immersive technology and immersive training tools, providing South African educational institutions with access to high-quality, industry-standard immersive training experiences.

Our goal is to help equip learners with practical industry knowledge and familiarity with complex equipment, processes and environments before they enter real-world facilities.

We aim to contribute to the growth of South Africa's technology sector through locally owned intellectual property, employment opportunities and connections between South African technical skills and international markets.

What sets the company apart

  1. The founder builds the products Over a decade of hands-on experience means no external development team is needed. Development costs stay low, iteration is fast, and the IP stays in the company.
  2. Real revenue already A paying international client means product development is supported by actual cash flow.
  3. A delivery record across major brands Work delivered for Anglo American, Aston Martin, Nissan, Absa, Investec, Sasol, Nestlé, Network Rail, Transnet, HP, Hasbro, and Roche.
  4. Working products, not concepts Super Immersive Homes has a large working codebase; LekkeLeer is live; WebAR demos are published; printed products are selling.
  5. Export-proven Revenue already comes from the UK; products are designed for both South African and international markets.

Job creation and local impact

  • Direct job creation: Create opportunities for local developers and technical specialists as products move into development and commercialisation.
  • Local skills development: Recruit graduates from the Mossel Bay 4IR campus and provide pathways into real commercial software, 3D, and immersive technology projects.
  • Export earnings: Build on existing UK contract revenue, bringing foreign income into the Garden Route while developing locally owned intellectual property.
  • Local economic impact: Develop products that reduce wasted design time, improve property and valuation workflows, reduce training costs through immersive simulation, support Foundation Phase literacy, and create new opportunities across property, tourism, and heritage sectors.
