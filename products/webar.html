AR books specifically

The Wild Wonders kids book demonstrates the full AR books product line: printed board-book layouts with page-marker WebAR that brings illustrations to life. This connects to the Hatched Books / RLP lineage in the company’s publishing work.

Revenue model

  • Custom AR book or packaging project for a publisher or brand (project fee).
  • Licensed WebAR template adapted for a client’s product (lower cost, faster delivery).
  • Ongoing hosting and content updates.
