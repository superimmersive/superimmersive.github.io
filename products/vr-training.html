Licensed product

Simulation-based training tools

Commercially delivered; being productised as a direct offering.

Licensed VR training tools for colleges, corporate training departments, and safety programmes. Modules cover practical skills that are expensive or risky to train in the real world — fire safety, electrical work, road safety, mechanical tasks, and industry-specific procedures.

Revenue model

  • Custom build for a client (project fee).
  • Licensed module repackaged and resold to similar institutions.
  • Ongoing content updates and analytics (SaaS layer).

Productisation plan

Move from bespoke studio delivery to a catalogue of licensed modules that institutions can purchase, configure, and deploy without a full custom build each time.

Next steps

  • Package the HRUC and Anglo American modules as reference products.
  • Define a standard licensing and deployment model.
  • Offer as a commissionable service alongside XR development work.
