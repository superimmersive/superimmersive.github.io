VR Driving-School Simulator

Documentation complete; prototype phase.

A virtual driving simulator built around South African driving standards. It gives learner drivers safe practice in a controlled VR environment before they get into a real car — covering road rules, hazard awareness, and manoeuvres relevant to the K53 testing framework.

What has been built

  • 16-chapter project document and condensed project overview.
  • Funding proposal prepared for institutional and grant applications.
  • Prototype phase — core VR interaction and driving mechanics in development.

Target customers

  • Driving schools and learner-driver training centres.
  • Corporate fleet training providers.
  • Government and NGO road-safety programmes.

Revenue model

  • Per-seat or per-site licensing.
  • Institutional subscriptions with usage analytics.
  • Sponsorship and branded training modules.

Next steps

  • Complete prototype with core driving scenarios.
  • Pilot with one driving school or training centre.
  • Serious development ramp planned after Super Immersive Homes commercialisation (18–36 month horizon).

Supporting documents available on request: Driving Simulator Condensed Project Document; Driving Simulator Funding Proposal.
