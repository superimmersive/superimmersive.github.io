Super Immersive Homes

Working product core; pre-revenue. Primary product.

It is not CAD. It is the conversation before CAD.

Someone is about to spend a lot of money on a house they cannot see. The architect draws. The client says that is not what I meant. Days disappear. Super Immersive Homes turns a brief or a rough plan into a layout you can edit and a room you can walk through, in the same sitting, until everyone says yes, that direction.

It does not produce council-ready drawings. It does not replace the architect.

First buyers are homeowners and small architecture practices. Agencies and developers come next. Property valuation firms and bank valuation units are a later API, once practices will say it in public.

What has been built

  • Detection pipeline: OpenCV + EasyOCR + optional PyTorch; FastAPI job system; structured JSON / SVG / Blender export.
  • Vector editor and 3D walkthrough: Vite + React + TypeScript + React Three Fiber — roughly 340 source files and 90,000 lines of application code.

Go-to-market

  1. Now Homeowners and small architecture practices (self-serve and assisted pilots).
  2. Next Agencies and developers using the same core as a delivery tool.
  3. Later Valuation firms and bank valuation units (API / platform), once the product is in production use.

We are deliberately not starting with banks and valuation firms. Their procurement takes months. A small practice can see value in the first hour.

Revenue model

Working numbers, being tested in interviews and first pilots before being locked. Year 1 is the business. Unlimited generation is not sold.

  • Homeowner project pass R399–R699 One project, 60 days. Layout options, 2D edit, 3D walkthrough, export to take to an architect or builder. One-off, not a subscription.
  • Practice pilot R60,000–R120,000 8–12 weeks, capped. Onboarding, branding, 2–5 users, about 10–20 projects, weekly support, review at the end.
  • Professional subscription R499–R999 / person / month After a pilot. Ongoing projects, monthly AI-generation allowance, commercial-use licence, client share links.
  • Small team R2,000–R5,000 / month 3–5 people. Shared templates, higher usage, basic org controls.

Homeowners do not subscribe monthly. Most people do not design houses every month.

The practice pilot is first real revenue. Sold as one capped number so the practice knows the commitment. AI generation is metered.

Enterprise API (valuation / portfolio systems) is a year 2+ offer, after there are case studies from practices. Twelve-month revenue target: R200,000–R600,000 — 1–2 paid practice pilots, at least one converting to subscription.

Connection to 3DGS

The platform can ingest 3D Gaussian Splatting captures of real buildings as the walkthrough layer — moving from “design a building” to “capture, edit, and present a real building.” See 3DGS capture and processing.
