LekkeLeer

MVP in early access.

LekkeLeer is a CAPS-aligned school learning platform focused on Foundation Phase reading and parent–school communication. It supports homework, reading practice, and progress tracking for classroom, homework, enrichment, and home-schooling use.

What has been built

  • Live MVP — Afrikaans Foundation Phase reading (Lees sinne, Term 1 Weeks 1–8).
  • iOS app — Expo build in early access alongside the web reader.
  • SchoolLink — parent school companion; reading homework opens Lees Saam.
  • Features: tap-to-hear, karaoke-style highlighting, read-aloud with speech recognition, progress saving.
