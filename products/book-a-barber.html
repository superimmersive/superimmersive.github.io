Marketplace

Book a Barber

Working interactive prototype. Pre-revenue.

An on-demand two-sided marketplace that connects clients who need a haircut now with barbers who are open nearby — at the shop or as a house call. Unlike appointment-based booking apps (Fresha, Booksy), Book a Barber is built for immediate availability: barber goes Open, client requests, barber accepts, proximity confirms the session, client pays in-app.

What has been built

  • Interactive dual-phone web demo — live and functional.
  • Core product loop: Open → discover → request → accept → meet → pay → rate.
  • React prototype with client and barber flows, service listings, and session management.

Try yourself

Dual-phone web demo — log in as client or barber and walk the full loop.

Open the live prototype superimmersive.github.io/book-a-barber

Target market

South African barbers and clients — starting in one city, expanding once density is proven.

Revenue model

  • Free for barbers in year one to build supply density.
  • About 10% platform service fee on completed paid jobs thereafter.
  • Long-term: same matching engine applied to other on-demand local services (nails, cleaning, garden work).
